# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

Welcome to the central hub for OctoAcme's project management processes. This repository contains the processes, workflows, templates, and best practices that guide how OctoAcme delivers projects consistently and effectively.

## Project Management at OctoAcme

OctoAcme delivers projects through a structured five-stage lifecycle, starting with **Initiation**—validating needs through a lightweight One-pager that confirms success metrics, stakeholders, and sponsor alignment—and moving through **Planning**, **Execution**, **Release**, and **Close/Retrospective**. The team operates with clear roles for Project Manager, Product Manager, Developers, and QA, ensuring defined responsibilities and robust accountability. Iterative delivery is supported via prioritized backlogs, sprint cycles, and review-focused workflows using GitHub Projects, with frequent standups, syncs, and transparent risk escalation.

Quality assurance is embedded at every stage: all features undergo automated testing, integration checks, security scanning, and manual QA where needed. Risks are actively identified and tracked in a Risk Register, with routine review during weekly syncs and a clear path for escalation (team-level → PM → Product Lead → Sponsor). Releases are governed by well-documented checklists, CI checks, release notes, and rollback plans to protect users and stakeholders. Pre-release requirements include all acceptance criteria met, security scans passing, smoke tests prepared, and incident response plans documented.

Communication strategies at OctoAcme foster alignment and transparency through a consistent cadence: daily standups (15 min), weekly delivery syncs, weekly PM-PdM alignment, monthly stakeholder updates, and ad-hoc escalations as needed. Regular status updates use standardized templates covering progress, next steps, risks, and decisions needed. Continuous improvement is institutionalized via retrospectives after each sprint or milestone—structured to identify what went well, what could improve, and actionable next steps with clear owners and due dates. Learnings become enhancements to processes and product culture, measured for impact.

All artifacts—including project one-pagers, planning documents, execution guidelines, risk registers, acceptance criteria, definitions of done, and retrospective notes—are versioned in this repository, providing a single source of truth for onboarding, day-to-day alignment, and institutional knowledge. This approach accelerates onboarding, reduces single-person dependencies, and ensures every team member has equal access to processes, decisions, and rationale.
